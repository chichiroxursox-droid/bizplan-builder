## ⚑ VIABILITY VERDICT: 🟡 YELLOW — viable, strong economics, two named risks

**This is nearly a GREEN.** The money math is excellent. It's held at YELLOW by two
concrete, plan-shaping risks (venue access in the target towns, and a hard seasonal gap),
both fixable — which is exactly why it's YELLOW and not RED.

### The margin arithmetic (numbers, not adjectives)
- **Cash margin ≈ 100%.** $25 (1-on-1) revenue − ~$0 COGS − ~$0 fixed = ~$25/lesson.
  Break-even is the *first lesson* (no fixed costs to cover). The interview anchor
  (~$130/mo take-home from 1–2 lessons/wk) reproduces from these inputs — model agrees
  with the bank account (rule 3 satisfied).
- **Labor-adjusted (the honest test).** A standalone $25 1-on-1 with ~50 min round-trip
  travel = ~1.83 paid-equivalent hours → **effective ~$13.6/hr**. Positive, above PA
  minimum ($7.25), but **below the local coach market wage of ~$32.86/hr**
  [VERIFIED — BLS Philly metro Coaches & Scouts mean $68,350/yr ÷ 2,080]. Batching 2–3
  lessons per park trip + repricing to $40–$50 lifts effective pay to ~$30–40/hr — at or
  above market. So the business *clears the market-wage bar* once priced and routed
  properly; it doesn't today. **Not a forced RED** (cash margin > 0; owner earns a
  positive wage — the below-market piece is a stated fact, not an auto-downgrade).
- **Demand vs. target.** Goal = 5 steady weekly clients. Target pool: Lower Merion has
  ~15,500 kids under 18 [VERIFIED], basketball is the #1 youth sport at 41.9%
  participation [VERIFIED] → ~6,500 basketball-playing kids in the affluent target area.
  5 clients = **~0.08%** of that pool. Break-even volume is trivially below demand
  evidence. And it was **nearly hit once already** (~5 clients last year).

### The 3 facts driving the verdict
1. **Zero-cost, ~100% cash margin, break-even at lesson one.** Owner-stated $0 COGS/fixed;
   anchor reconciles. Every pricing/volume gain drops almost entirely to take-home.
2. **Ample, affluent, verified demand.** Lower Merion median household income **$176,512**
   [VERIFIED — 2.2× the US median], ~15,500 under-18, basketball the #1 youth sport.
   Cost-intensive private-pay sports skew to higher-income families (20.2-pt gap)
   [VERIFIED] — so the Main Line targeting is strategically correct.
3. **The service is underpriced.** $25/hr 1-on-1 sits **below the market floor**: verified
   Philadelphia range $30–$150/hr, national beginner tier $30–$60/hr, entry $30–$50.
   $35-for-two = **$17.50/player** vs. a $30–$50/player small-group market. Repricing is a
   near-free growth lever.

### Strongest disconfirming fact (the evidence-against pass)
**Lower Merion Township ordinance:** *"It shall be unlawful for private individuals or
groups to engage in … profit-making … activities in any Township park without a permit,
which will only be issued to community-based organizations"* [VERIFIED — ecode360 Ch.109].
The coach's **entire advertised service area** (Ardmore, Bryn Mawr, Narberth, Bala Cynwyd,
Wynnewood, Merion Station, Gladwyne, Villanova — all Lower Merion / Haverford / Radnor)
consists of townships that bar solo paid coaching in their **public parks**. Paired with
an **outdoor-only model that's non-viable Dec–Mar** (Philadelphia stays below freezing all
day ~17–23 days/mo in deep winter [VERIFIED]) during the peak school-basketball season,
and a coach who **pauses for their own winter season** (the exact cause of last year's
collapse from ~5 clients to 1–2) — **delivery, not demand, is the real constraint.**

### The single fact that would have made it RED
If **Philadelphia** parks were *also* closed to individual paid-instruction permits **and**
no private-court/home delivery path existed, a zero-cost service would have **nowhere legal
to operate** → RED. It isn't: Philadelphia **does** issue recreation/athletic permits to
individuals (~10-day approval) [VERIFIED], and lessons at a client's home/driveway hoop or
a private/school court sit outside the township-park ban entirely. A legal venue path
exists → **YELLOW, not RED.**

## Findings by dimension (all tagged)

### 1. Competitor & market pricing — $25/hr is below the market floor
- 1-on-1 Philadelphia: **$30–$150/hr** [VERIFIED — Lessons.com Philly, src 1].
- National tiers: entry **$30–$50**, experienced **$60–$90**, elite **$100–$150+**
  [VERIFIED — TeachMe.To, src 2]; beginner tier **$30–$60/hr** [VERIFIED — Lessons.com
  cost guide, src 3].
- Small group priced **per player**: 2–4 kids **$30–$50/player**, 4+ kids **$20–$40/player**
  [VERIFIED — src 1]. Coach's $35-for-two = **$17.50/player** → well under market.
- Local trainers list **"Contact for price"** (Rise Hoops, etc.) [VERIFIED — src 1] — the
  Main Line market is **price-opaque**, no public competitor sticker to undercut you.
- Takeaway: **$25/hr undercuts even the $30 beginner floor by ~17%** and the experienced
  tier by 60–70%. Biggest, cheapest lever on the board.

### 2. Local demand — deep, affluent, verified

### 3. Coach's labor wage (to price your time)
- Philadelphia metro Coaches & Scouts: **mean $68,350/yr** (~$32.86/hr equiv); BLS
  suppresses the hourly figure [VERIFIED — src 9]. National: median **$45,910/yr**
  (~$22.07/hr), 10th pct **$27,040** (~$13/hr) [VERIFIED — src 10].
- Your **effective** rate today (travel-loaded, standalone) ≈ **$13.6/hr** — roughly the
  national 10th percentile. Repricing + batching fixes this.

### 4. Regulatory — no capital wall, but a real venue constraint + a trust must-do
- **Lower Merion parks: solo for-profit coaching NOT permittable** (permits only to
  community orgs) [VERIFIED — src 11]. ← the venue wall in your target towns.
- **Philadelphia parks: paid instruction permit required but issued to individuals**,
  ~10-day approval [VERIFIED — src 12]; exact fee `[ASK]`.
- **PA child-abuse clearances (Act 153/CPSL):** required for those working with children
  [VERIFIED — src 13]. Costs: PA Criminal History **$10**, Child Abuse **$13** (free for
  volunteers), FBI fingerprint **$27.50** (often waived first-time) [VERIFIED — src 14].
  ~**$50 total** — inside your budget, and a **trust/credibility asset** to display.
- **Philadelphia:** Commercial Activity License **$0** [VERIFIED — src 15]; BIRT applies,
  $100k exemption removed for 2025 [VERIFIED — src 16].
- **Liability insurance:** sports/fitness general liability avg **$29/mo (~$350/yr)**
  [VERIFIED — src 17]. Optional now; worth it as you scale.

### 5. Customer acquisition — your ad economics are unusually good
- Your real Google Ads: **$0.56 CPC**, well under the **Sports & Rec $2.77** benchmark and
  all-industry **$5.42** [VERIFIED — src 18]. You're buying clicks cheap.
- Sports & Rec Google CPL **$44.26**, conversion **7.69%** [VERIFIED — src 18] — your ~$41
  cost-per-client is right on benchmark.
- **Facebook is the cheaper, scalable channel** (already your organic workhorse): Sports &
  Rec lead CPL **$19.30**, CPC **$1.07**; traffic-objective CPC **$0.41** [VERIFIED — src 19].
- Landing-page conversion benchmark **~6.6%** [VERIFIED — src 20].
- Implication: at ~$41/client with strong retention, **$90/mo can realistically add 3–4
  steady weekly clients over a year.** `[ASK]` your true lead→client close rate + monthly
  local search volume to firm this up.

### 6. Evidence-against — churn, seasonality, an indoor competitor
- Basketball = **lowest-spend youth sport, $427/yr** per family [VERIFIED — src 23] — a
  ceiling on willingness to pay.
- Kids typically **quit their sport by age 11** [VERIFIED — src 23]; you train the
  youngest = **highest-churn** cohort. Retention work matters. (Note: an earlier draft
  cited "36% quit entirely / 15% switch" as VERIFIED — those specific figures are NOT on
  the cited page and have been removed. Corrected 2026-07-23.)
- **Winter:** outdoor training non-viable much of **Dec–Mar** [VERIFIED — src 22] — the
  peak school-basketball season and the exact window that pulls you into your own season.
- **Indoor competitor in your target town:** Danny Cooper Basketball trains year-round at
  AFC Fitness, **Bala Cynwyd** [VERIFIED — src 24] — neutralizes an outdoor-only model in
  winter.

### 7. Website / funnel audit — good bones, three fixable gaps
- Site clearly lists **$25 single / $35 for two ($17.50 each)**, 1-hr, no packages
  [VERIFIED — src 26]; service areas = the 10 Main Line towns [VERIFIED — src 29];
  parent-friendly "safe & welcome" framing, references on request, no-risk first lesson
  [VERIFIED — src 28].
- **Gap 1 — no live booking:** contact is text/call/email only; self-scheduler "being set
  up" [VERIFIED — src 27]. Every click that doesn't text you is lost.
- **Gap 2 — no trust proof:** no clearances/background-check/CPR/insurance shown on a
  *youth* service [VERIFIED — src 28]. Highest-trust buyers (Main Line parents) want this.
- **Gap 3 — low search visibility:** business doesn't surface in a branded web search;
  GBP visibility weak `[BENCHMARK]`. (Interview: GBP exists but Ads↔GBP link broke.)
