# Winter-Proof It — one page

**Start when:** You have 5 steady clients — OR it's **8 weeks before December**, whichever
comes first. (This is the move that beat you last year. Don't wait.)

**Goal of this phase:** Keep your clients through winter and your own season, so you never
have to rebuild from scratch again.

## Do this
1. **Move winter sessions to private hoops.** Push families toward their **own driveway or
   garage hoop**; for those without one, book **weekend indoor slots**. This keeps you off
   Lower Merion public parks (where solo paid coaching is barred [VERIFIED]) and out of the
   Dec–March freeze [VERIFIED].
2. **Approach one indoor facility** (a gym, church hall, or rec center) about renting a few
   weekend hours for winter. Even 2–3 hours/week protects your core clients.
3. **Plug your own-season hole:** 4 weeks before your season, offer regulars a **reduced
   winter schedule** (early mornings/weekends) or line up a **trusted sub** so no one drifts
   to the year-round competitor in Bala Cynwyd [VERIFIED].
4. **Sell a "winter package"** (e.g. 8 sessions prepaid) — locks in commitment before the
   cold and smooths your cash.

## The one metric to watch
**Client retention through Dec–March.** You win this phase if you **keep 4 of your 5**
clients into spring [ASK — your target] instead of rebuilding from 1–2.

## Avoid this phase
Going quiet over winter and "restarting in spring." That's the exact pattern that dropped
you from 5 clients to 1–2 — a lost regular is usually gone for good. Stay in front of them,
even at reduced volume.
