# Fix the Foundation — one page

**Start when:** Now. (Costs almost nothing; everything else depends on it.)

**Goal of this phase:** Charge what you're worth and make it dead-easy to book you and
trust you — *before* spending another dollar on ads.

## Do this
1. **Raise your price to $40/hr** (1-on-1) and **$60 for two** ($30/kid). Text existing
   families a friendly 2–3 week heads-up. You'll still be at the market's *entry* floor
   ($30–$50/hr [VERIFIED]) — you are not overcharging.
2. **Get your PA clearances** (~$50 total: Child Abuse $13, State Police $10, FBI ~$27.50
   often waived [VERIFIED]) and **put "Cleared & background-checked" on your website's
   About page** (it shows none right now [VERIFIED]).
3. **Add a one-tap booking link** to your site (a free Calendly-style scheduler). Today it
   only says "text me" [VERIFIED] — every click that doesn't text you is a lost customer.
4. **Reactivate your Google Business Profile** link (the Ads warning flagged it broken) —
   it's your #1 free local channel.

## The one metric to watch
**Your price on the website = $40** and **a working booking link is live.** Advance to the
next phase the day both are true and clearances are ordered.

## Avoid this phase
Spending on ads while your site still can't take a booking and shows no credentials. You'd
be paying to send trusting Main Line parents to a page that can't close them.
