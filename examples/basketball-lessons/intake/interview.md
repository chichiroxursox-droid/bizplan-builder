**Track (Q0):** Existing business, growing it. Owner describes it as "really small."

**What it is:** 1-on-1 basketball training for younger kids. Owner travels to a park
near the client. 1-hour sessions. Marketed locally.
**Location:** Owner based in a West Philadelphia neighborhood on the city line.
Brand name "Main Line Basketball Lessons" targets the affluent Main Line suburbs
just west (Merion, Bala Cynwyd, Lower Merion, Wynnewood, Ardmore). Note the gap:
based in a city neighborhood, branded to the wealthier suburbs next door.
**Website:** https://main-line-basketball-lessons.vercel.app/

**Outcome (Q1 — 3-year win):** At least 5 consistent clients per week, averaging
$100–200/week in revenue. (Owner clarified: clients per week, not per day.)
