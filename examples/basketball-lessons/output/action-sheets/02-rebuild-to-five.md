# Rebuild to Five — one page

**Start when:** Your price is $40, your booking link works, and clearances are ordered
(Sheet 1 done).

**Goal of this phase:** Get back to your proven ~5 steady clients — the number you already
hit once — now at $40. Repriced, that's ~$140/wk (inside your goal); once each of the 5
trains *weekly*, you reach the ~$200/wk top.

## Do this
1. **Run Facebook + Google Ads at ~$90/mo total.** Facebook first — Sports & Rec leads run
   **~$19/lead** [VERIFIED] and it's already your best organic channel. Keep Google going
   at your real **$0.56/click** (5× cheaper than typical [VERIFIED]).
2. **Point every ad at the booking link**, and lead with two lines: "Cleared &
   background-checked coach" + "First lesson risk-free."
3. **Ask your current regulars for one referral each.** Referrals cost ~$0 and close
   fastest. A free session for a successful referral pays for itself.
4. **Post 2 short clips a week** (a drill, a kid's before/after) to the Facebook page —
   free proof that feeds the ads.

## The one metric to watch
**Steady regular clients.** Advance to the next phase when you hit **5 regulars two weeks
running** [ASK — your target] (~$140/wk at $40, or ~$200/wk if all 5 train weekly).
*Stop-and-fix trigger:* if ~60 ad clicks bring **0** inquiries, the landing page — not the
ad — is broken. Fix the page before spending more.

## Avoid this phase
Chasing one-off sessions. One weekly regular is worth a dozen one-and-dones — kids
typically quit their sport by age 11 [VERIFIED], so a client you lose is usually gone for
good. Book *recurring* slots.
